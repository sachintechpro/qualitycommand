METHOD & TRANSPARENCY

Quality governance principles

01

Protect release gates

Sev-1 defects, unapproved changes, coverage gaps, and unsigned UAT remain visible.

02

Respect shared capacity

Staffing changes must account for their impact across all simultaneous releases.

03

Separate assumptions from evidence

Modeled improvements require confirmation before they become release commitments.

04

Document decisions

Expose remaining risks, accountable actions, and management approvals.
